## Profile hierarchy

1. **Foundation role family:** Machine Learning Engineer.
2. **Technical focus:** Data Engineering and data-centric ML systems.
3. **Future specialization:** AI Reliability / Data & AI Reliability Engineering.
4. **GenAI positioning:** cross-cutting engineering competency required for competitive engineering work; not a standalone target profile.

## Current positioning

Canonical current wording:

> Machine Learning Engineer with strong Data Engineering and Reliability focus.

German working wording:

> Machine Learning Engineer mit starkem Data-Engineering- und Reliability-Fokus.

The future-direction label `AI Reliability Engineer` or `Data & AI Reliability Engineer` describes the intended specialization path. It must not be presented as an already-held formal title without evidence.

## Pipeline implications

- Primary search and matching family: Machine Learning Engineering.
- Strong supporting signals: Python, SQL, data pipelines, data platforms, data quality, MLOps, evaluation, observability, governance and controlled automation.
- Future-direction signals: AI reliability, AI quality, model/agent evaluation, drift control, evidence chains, AI observability, AI assurance and safe agent systems.
- GenAI terms are competency and system-context signals. They are insufficient by themselves to define a target role.
- Data Engineering remains a deliberate focus and bridge, not a competing canonical profile.
- Vacancy-specific wording may vary, but the hierarchy must remain unchanged.

## Boundaries

- Do not infer seniority, production experience or formal job titles from the target direction.
- Do not create a separate GenAI-only profile.
- Do not let LLM-generated wording overwrite canonical facts or hard constraints.
- Runtime, search-term, matching and CV changes require versioned implementation and validation under `DOC-018`.
